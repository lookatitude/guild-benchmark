Keyboard, focus, names, no inference

  • Tests: every job control is reached by Tab. Blocked controls use aria-disabled and keep focus through polls. Enter on a blocked control does nothing. The panel is a named region.
  • Tests: you can accept or reject a recommendation with the keyboard only. The confirm group is named "Confirm accept" and states the effect before you confirm. No "implemented" or "filed" claim is shown.
  • Tests: absent evidence shows "not reported", "Not reported" or "unknown". It never shows 0, a %, or "yes". Unobserved usage with a stored 0 shows "unknown".
  • Browser: Tab goes nav → back link → evidence <summary> on Run pages, and nav → the three selects on Compare. No horizontal overflow at 320px on any of the 6 pages (scrollWidth == innerWidth).
